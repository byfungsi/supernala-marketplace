# Set up Maple

Maple lets your Bots read traces, logs, metrics, errors and dashboards from your Maple organization. This version only reads. You sign in with Maple, so there are no keys to create.

## In Supernala

1. Open **Marketplace → Apps → Maple** and click **Install**.
2. Click **Connect account**, sign in to Maple and allow access to your organization.
3. Choose which Bots can use Maple and click **Save changes**.

## If something goes wrong

- **Data from another organization is missing**: each connection covers the organization you chose at sign-in. Add another account for a different organization.
- **Asked to reconnect**: Maple ended the sign-in. Connect again.
