# Set up Tebar

Tebar lets your Bots publish and schedule posts, manage the media library and ideas board, read and answer comments, check insights, and, on plans with Inbox, answer Instagram DMs and manage automations. Connecting social accounts stays in Tebar.

## Before you start

Sign in to [Tebar](https://tebar.app) and connect the social accounts your Bots should use. Inbox and automation tools need a Tebar plan with Inbox (Starter, Basic, Pro or Agency).

## In Supernala

1. Open **Marketplace → Apps → Tebar** and click **Install**.
2. Click **Connect account**. Sign in to Tebar and choose **Baca dan tulis** (read and write) on the access page. Supernala needs both; read only is refused.
3. Choose which Bots can use Tebar and click **Save changes**. Posting, replies, DMs and other changes still ask for your approval.

## If something goes wrong

- **Connection refused after sign-in**: you chose read only. Connect again and choose **Baca dan tulis**.
- **A social account is missing**: connect it in Tebar first; Bots see only accounts already in your Tebar account.
- **Inbox or automation tools fail**: your Tebar plan has no Inbox. See [Tebar plans](https://docs.tebar.app/paket).
- **Asked to reconnect**: Tebar ended the sign-in, for example after a password reset or when access was revoked under API key → Connected apps. Connect again.
