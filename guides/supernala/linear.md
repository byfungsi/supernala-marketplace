# Set up Linear

Linear lets your Bots read and search issues, projects and cycles, and create or update them after you approve. You sign in with Linear, so there are no keys to create.

## In Supernala

1. Open **Marketplace → Apps → Linear** and click **Install**.
2. Click **Connect account**, sign in to Linear and allow read and write access for your workspace.
3. Choose which Bots can use Linear and click **Save changes**. Changes still ask for your approval.

## If something goes wrong

- **A team is missing**: Bots see only what your Linear account can see.
- **Asked to reconnect**: Linear ended the sign-in, for example after an admin revoked the authorization. Connect again.
