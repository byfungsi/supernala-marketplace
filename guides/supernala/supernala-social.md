# Set up Supernala Social

Supernala Social lets your Bots publish and schedule posts to your Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts, Threads profiles, Discord channels and LinkedIn Pages, read and answer inbox comments and direct messages, check analytics (including posts you published outside Supernala Social), and review recent activity, in your personal Social workspace and any shared workspaces you belong to. Posts carry text with up to ten photos and videos. Facebook Pages and LinkedIn Pages take several photos or one video. Connecting accounts, workspace administration and TikTok posting stay in the Social app.

Supernala Social is a separate product with its own sign-in, so you approve the connection on the Social side.

## Before you start

Open [Supernala Social](https://social.supernala.com), sign in with the same Google account, and connect the social accounts your Bots should use.

## In Supernala

1. Open **Marketplace → Apps → Supernala Social** and click **Install**.
2. Click **Connect account**. Supernala Social opens; sign in if asked, check the request and click **Approve**.
3. Choose which Bots can use Supernala Social and click **Save changes**. Publishing, replies, moderation and cancelling a scheduled post still ask for your approval.

## If something goes wrong

- **A social account is missing**: connect it in Supernala Social first. Bots see only accounts already in your Social workspaces.
- **Comments, messages or analytics say they aren't enabled yet**: they are still being rolled out and open for your account or workspace later.
- **A Bot can read a shared workspace but not post**: you are a viewer there. A workspace admin can make you a member.
- **Asked to reconnect**: the connection was disconnected under **AI apps → Connected apps** in Supernala Social, or your sign-in ended. Connect again.
