# Set up PostHog

PostHog lets your Bots query analytics and manage items such as dashboards, insights and feature flags in your PostHog projects. Every action asks for your approval. You sign in with PostHog, so there are no keys to create.

## In Supernala

1. Open **Marketplace → Apps → PostHog** and click **Install**.
2. Click **Connect account**, sign in to PostHog and allow the requested access.
3. Choose which Bots can use PostHog and click **Save changes**.

## If something goes wrong

- **A project is missing**: Bots see only the organizations and projects your PostHog account can access.
- **Asked to reconnect**: PostHog ended the sign-in. Connect again.
