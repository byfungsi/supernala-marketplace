# Set up Repliz

Repliz lets your Bots work with the social accounts and content you manage in Repliz. Connecting new social accounts stays in Repliz.

## Before you start

Open [Repliz → Settings → API](https://repliz.com/user/setting/api) and copy your **Access Key** and **Secret Key**. You paste them on the Repliz sign-in page in the next step; keep them private.

## In Supernala

1. Open **Marketplace → Apps → Repliz** and click **Install**.
2. Click **Connect account**. On the Repliz sign-in page, paste your Access Key and Secret Key and click **Connect**. Supernala keeps only the sign-in Repliz gives back, not your keys.
3. Choose which Bots can use Repliz and click **Save changes**. Changes still ask for your approval.

## If something goes wrong

- **Sign-in rejected**: copy both keys again from [Repliz → Settings → API](https://repliz.com/user/setting/api), or generate new ones there.
- **A social account is missing**: connect it in Repliz first; Bots see only accounts already in your Repliz workspace.
- **Asked to reconnect**: Repliz ended the sign-in. Connect again.
