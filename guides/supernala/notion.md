# Set up Notion

Notion lets your Bots search and read the pages and databases you share with it. This version only reads; it does not change your workspace. You sign in with Notion, so there are no keys to create.

## In Supernala

1. Open **Marketplace → Apps → Notion** and click **Install**.
2. Click **Connect account**, sign in to Notion and choose the workspace.
3. On Notion's screen, choose the pages Supernala can access. Bots can only read what you select.
4. Choose which Bots can use Notion and click **Save changes**.

## If something goes wrong

- **A page is missing**: it was not selected during sign-in. Connect again and include it, or share it with the connection from Notion.
- **Asked to reconnect**: Notion ended the sign-in. Connect again.
