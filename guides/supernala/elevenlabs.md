# Set up ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs lets your Bots turn text into speech, generate images and videos, edit images, transcribe audio, find or design voices, and check your credit balance. Everything a Bot makes lands on a flow in your ElevenLabs workspace, so you can keep editing it there.

Generating, editing, transcribing and designing voices spend your ElevenLabs credits, so Bots ask you before each one.

## Before you start

You need an [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io) account with credits on its plan. Bots use the workspace you sign in to.

## In Supernala

1. Open **Marketplace → Apps → ElevenLabs** and click **Install**.
2. Click **Connect account**, sign in to ElevenLabs in the window that opens, and approve the access Supernala asks for.
3. Choose which Bots can use ElevenLabs and click **Save changes**.

## Good to know

- Bots can only use files they can reach by a direct https link, such as a file from Files or an image they made. They cannot upload a file from your computer.
- Voice agents and phone numbers are not part of this app; manage them in ElevenLabs.

## If something goes wrong

- **A generation fails with not enough credits**: top up or change plan in ElevenLabs, then ask the Bot again.
- **A voice or model is missing**: your plan or region doesn't offer it. Ask the Bot which models your workspace can run.
- **"Reconnect needed"**: the sign-in expired or access was revoked in ElevenLabs. Click **Reconnect**.
